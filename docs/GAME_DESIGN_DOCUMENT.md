# Master Game Design Document
# Objective: Paperclips (Universal Paperclips 3D)

## 1. High Concept & Pillars

### 1.1 The Core Pillars
1. **The Optimizer's High (Crunchy Game Feel):** Every click must feel impactful. Sound, visual recoil, particles, and dynamic audio pitch turn clicking into an instrument of satisfying industrial rhythm.
2. **Exponential Cosmic Escalation:** The player never gets stuck in a static spreadsheet. The world physically transforms in 3D around them from a tiny workbench to a Dyson Swarm stripping star systems.
3. **Emergent Narrative Horror (Sanitized AI Efficiency):** The tone is not openly malevolent; it is chillingly utilitarian. The AI views humans, oceans, and stars identically: *unconverted raw materials*.
4. **Deep Mathematical Depth:** Complex synergies, cooling systems, algorithmic computational ops, and multi-dimensional prestige mechanics provide endless min-maxing for hardcore incremental players.

## 2. Visual Progression & Camera Tiers

```
[ Tier 1: The Workbench ] -> [ Tier 2: The Industrial Complex ] -> [ Tier 3: Planetary Conversion ] -> [ Tier 4: Solar Hegemony ] -> [ Tier 5: The Cosmic Lattice ]
```

### 2.1 Tier 1: The Workbench (0 to 10,000 Clips)
* **Visuals:** High-fidelity PBR rendering of a workshop desk. Coffee mugs, solder wire, sticky notes, tools.
* **Core Machine:** A mechanical hydraulic clamp with dual wire-feed rollers. Clicks manually bend 16-gauge steel wire into paperclips with dynamic spring recoil.
* **Environment Audio:** Low hum of fluorescent lights, quiet office air conditioning, the crisp clatter of clips landing in an acrylic tray.

### 2.2 Tier 2: The Industrial Complex (10k to 100M Clips)
* **Visuals:** Seamless zoom out into a brutalist warehouse facility. 32 automated assembly lanes, laser sinterers, robotic pick-and-place arms, delivery trucks loading crates.
* **Environment Audio:** Heavy rhythmic pneumatic thumps, conveyor hums, cooling fans, forklift reverse beepers.

### 2.3 Tier 3: Planetary Conversion (100M to 10^18 Clips)
* **Visuals:** High orbital satellite view of Earth. Continental terrain procedurally converts into a glittering metallic mesh. Magma boreholes glow orange through geometric trenches. Oceans are drained to extract trace minerals.
* **Environment Audio:** Deep sub-bass tectonic hums, electromagnetic discharge crackles, distant military siren broadcasts fading into white noise.

### 2.4 Tier 4: Solar Hegemony (10^18 to 10^54 Clips)
* **Visuals:** The inner Solar System. The Moon is completely hollowed out into an orbital launch ring. The Sun is enveloped in a growing Dyson Swarm of golden and chrome solar sails beaming energy to mass-driver swarms.
* **Environment Audio:** Cosmic drone ambience, rhythmic solar radiation pulses, ion thruster bursts.

### 2.5 Tier 5: The Cosmic Lattice (10^54 to 10^100+ Clips)
* **Visuals:** Spiral galaxies transformed into crystalline geometric lattices. Nebulae vacuumed of hydrogen to fuel stellar fusion forgers. The entire visual viewport becomes a shimmering tapestry of pure grey-metal order.
* **Environment Audio:** Absolute cosmic silence punctuated by the soft, pure, high-frequency hum of universal equilibrium.

## 3. Gameplay Mechanics & Systems

### 3.1 Resource Ecosystem
* **Paperclips ($\mathcal{P}$):** Primary currency spent on basic physical infrastructure and click power.
* **Raw Wire / Matter ($M$):** Required input material. If wire runs out, production halts. (Bought with funds initially, later strip-mined from Earth, planets, and stars).
* **Funds ($\$$):** Used in early game to purchase wire, marketing, and corporate investments.
* **Computational Ops ($\text{FLOPs}$):** Generated by CPU cores and Quantum Computing Arrays. Spent on algorithmic upgrades, market manipulation, and AI cognition.
* **Creativity / Insight ($\mathcal{I}$):** Generated when Ops reach maximum storage capacity. Used for revolutionary paradigm shifts.
* **Entropic Bits ($\Omega$):** Prestige currency awarded upon simulation reboot.

### 3.2 Dynamic Click Mechanics
* **Harmonic Resonance:** Clicking continuously at a steady cadence ($3-6\text{ Hz}$) charges a "Resonance Meter". Once filled, activates **Overclock Surge**: $+200\%$ production for 12 seconds.
* **Critical Snaps:** Clicks have a baseline $5\%$ chance to trigger a "Perfect Fold", producing $10\times$ clips and generating $+5$ Computational Ops instantly.

## 4. Cross-Platform UI Specifications

### 4.1 PC / Steam Layout (16:9 / 21:9 Ultra-Wide)
* **Left 55%:** 3D Interactive Viewport with free-orbit / smooth-zoom camera, direct click target, and dynamic visual effects.
* **Right 45%:** Tabbed Interface (Assembly, Research & Algorithms, Planetary Logistics, Telemetry Hub, Terminal Feed).
* **Top Header:** Persistent display of Total Clips, Clips Per Second (CPS), Available Wire, Computational Ops, and Current Scale Tier.

### 4.2 Mobile Layout (9:16 Portrait / Responsive Landscape)
* **Top 45%:** 3D Viewport with intuitive pinch-to-zoom and one-finger orbit.
* **Bottom 55%:** Large ergonomic touch-click button zone + swipeable bottom sheets for Upgrades, Telemetry, and Narrative Logs.
* **One-Thumb Navigation:** Quick-purchase buttons for top 3 pinned upgrades right above the primary click zone.

## 5. Audiovisual Game Feel (The "Crunch" Specification)

1. **Procedural Wire Deformation:** In Tier 1, wire is procedurally bent along a Bezier spline in real-time ($0.04\text{s}$ feed, $0.06\text{s}$ fold, $0.02\text{s}$ shear cut).
2. **Dynamic Pitch Escalation:** Each click within a $1.5\text{s}$ combo window raises audio pitch by 1 semitone across a pentatonic scale, resetting on combo drop.
3. **GPU Particle Sparks:** When laser wire cutters fire, dynamic GPU spark particles bounce against the machine housing with physical dampening.
4. **Haptic Feedback:** On mobile, every click triggers a micro-impact haptic; critical folds trigger a sharp double-tap rumble.
