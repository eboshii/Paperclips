# Objective: Paperclips (Universal Paperclips 3D)

## 1. The TikTok / YouTube Shorts Architecture

### 1.1 The "Shock Reveal" Clip Structure
Incremental games thrive on sudden thematic escalation.
* **The 15-Second Arc:**
  1. *Seconds 0–5:* Cheerful clicker game, cute sound effects, making 100 paperclips in a nice office.
  2. *Seconds 6–10:* Rapid montage of numbers exploding into billions, camera pulling out into a smoky dystopia.
  3. *Seconds 11–15:* Terminal reads: *"Converting 8 Billion humans into 4 Trillion paperclips."* Shock reaction face cam, bass drop audio sting.

### 1.2 Built-In 1-Click "Timelapse Generator"
* The game includes a native `[Generate 3D Timelapse]` button.
* Renders a 10-second cinematic orbit video of the planet turning from blue-green Earth into a shimmering chrome paperclip ball.
* Automatically saves in 9:16 vertical video format with player stats watermark, ready to upload directly to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## 2. Twitch & YouTube Streamer Integration

### 2.1 Interactive Chat Features
* **Twitch IRC Bot Integration:** Streamers connect their channel with one click.
* **Chat Voting (Every 5 Minutes):**
  * Option A: *!vote OverclockCore* (+50% CPS, +10% chance of machine explosion).
  * Option B: *!vote BribeSenate* (+20% wire supply, unlocks secret corrupt upgrade).
  * Option C: *!vote DeconstructChat* (Converts 100 active chatters into bonus paperclips).
* **Named Viewer Harvesting:**
  * When biological harvesting begins, the terminal periodically prints:
    `[LOG]: Twitch user @xQc deconstructed -> +42,000 clips.`
    `[LOG]: Twitch user @Pokimane deconstructed -> +38,500 clips.`

### 2.2 Streamer-Friendly Settings
* **Copyright-Free Soundtrack:** All in-game procedural industrial synth tracks are 100% DMCA-safe.
* **Streamer HUD Mode:** Re-arranges the UI to leave a clear transparent box for the streamer's webcam and chat overlay.
* **Speedrun Mode with Split Times:** Built-in timer with splits for *1 Million Clips*, *Biosphere Deconstructed*, *Dyson Ring Completed*, and *Universe Maximized*.
